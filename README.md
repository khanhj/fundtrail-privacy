# Privacy Policy for Fund Trail

**Last Updated:** October 10, 2026

Fund Trail ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. Fund Trail is a free, ad-free mobile utility application designed to help users monitor Tron (TRX) blockchain transactions, verify wallet addresses, and enhance on-chain security.

### 1. Our Core Privacy Principles
* **100% Free & No Ads:** Fund Trail does not contain advertisements, trackers, or monetization algorithms.
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide personal details (such as your name, email, or phone number) to use the app.
* **Non-Custodial & Read-Only:** Fund Trail NEVER requests, handles, or stores your private keys, seed phrases, or wallet passwords. The app cannot initiate transactions or spend your funds.
* **Local Storage:** All preferences, watched addresses, and custom address book labels remain strictly on your device.

### 2. Information We Collect and Process
* **Personal Data:** We do not collect or share any personal identifying information (PII).
* **Public Blockchain Queries:** To display transaction history and address security statuses, the app queries public Tron blockchain nodes and explorers (such as TronGrid or public block APIs). These queries involve only public blockchain addresses and do not identify you as an individual.
* **Device Permissions:** The app requests only the minimum essential permissions necessary for functionality (such as network access to query the blockchain, and optional notifications for local transaction alerts). No background location or sensitive personal permissions are requested.

### 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties
We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Data is only exchanged with public blockchain APIs to retrieve public ledger information requested by the user.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Fund Trail is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
